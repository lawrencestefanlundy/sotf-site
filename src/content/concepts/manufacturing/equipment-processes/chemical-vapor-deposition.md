## Market structure

- CVD equipment is the **largest deposition modality**: roughly $24-27B on the wider market definitions, within deposition equipment overall at $22.5-25.9B (2025, narrow definition) to ~$63B (widest, 2024). Growth ~7-8% CAGR. Sources triangulated on **Deposition Materials Process Market** (DataIntelo, AnalystView, MRF/Zion).
- Oligopoly: **Applied Materials** (Producer, Centura; Black Diamond low-k), **Lam Research** (VECTOR PECVD, Striker ALD/CVD, ALTUS tungsten/molybdenum CVD), **Tokyo Electron Tel**, with **Asm International** in epi/ALD-adjacent CVD and **Aixtron** near-monopoly (~70%) in compound-semi MOCVD.
- The precursor layer below the tools: ~$1.7B (2024) growing ~10.4%, Merck/EMD ~30% on the advanced cut, top-5 ~58% (TECHCET, triangulated within ~20% by two 2026 secondaries; see the spine page).
- Low-k dielectrics ride CVD: production SiCOH (Black Diamond II, k ~2.5) is PECVD; the sub-2nm question is whether solid low-k survives at all against air gaps and amorphous BN ([Low-K Dielectrics](/materials/functional-layers/low-k-dielectrics/)).
