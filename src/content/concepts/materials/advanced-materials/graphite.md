## Physics / mechanism

Graphite is the layered sp2 carbon allotrope: covalently bonded honeycomb sheets held together by van der Waals interactions, with properties dominated by the anisotropy between in-plane and out-of-plane bonding. The stacking sequence of those sheets is a distinct degree of freedom. The weak interlayer bonding admits guest species between sheets, forming graphite intercalation compounds.
