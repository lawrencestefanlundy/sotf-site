A technique for generating **record-low-noise microwave/mmWave** signals by transferring the stability of a high-quality **optical reference** down to the RF domain through an [Optical Frequency Comb](/photonic-systems/light-sources/optical-frequency-comb/). Because the division reduces phase noise by the square of the division factor, OFD achieves spectral purity beyond other approaches (integrated, chip-scale demonstrations in Nature 2024 — see <sup class="ref"><a href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07057-0" title="Photonic mmWave / microwave signal generation — research landscape (2024–2025)" rel="noopener">ref</a></sup>).
