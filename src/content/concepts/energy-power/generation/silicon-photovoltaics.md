Incumbent crystalline-silicon solar cell technology — the commodity baseline that any new PV entrant (perovskite tandems, thin-film) must beat on $/W and bankability. In the learning loop it is a **suppressed sourcing lane** (crowded, commodity, incumbent-locked): the sourcing signal points away from pure silicon-PV plays and toward the tandem/materials edge tracked in **Perovskite Silicon Tandem Wins New Build Utility Pv**.
