The ABX3 crystal-structure material class. Metal-halide perovskites (MAPbI3, FAPbI3) are the leading next-generation photovoltaic absorber, prized for high efficiency at low process cost and for silicon-tandem stacks; the open barrier is operational stability. Also pursued as a semiconducting / optoelectronic substrate. The material under the **Next Gen Photovoltaics** and perovskite-silicon-tandem theses.
