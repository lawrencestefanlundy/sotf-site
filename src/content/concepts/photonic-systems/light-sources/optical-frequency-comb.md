A light source whose spectrum is a set of **evenly, precisely spaced** discrete frequency lines — a ruler in frequency space. Combs underpin precision metrology, optical clocks, spectroscopy, and increasingly **photonic RF/mmWave generation**: stabilising laser frequency/phase and enabling [Optical Frequency Division (OFD)](/communications/wireless/optical-frequency-division/).
