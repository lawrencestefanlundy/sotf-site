A microwave-photonic oscillator that produces **ultra-low-phase-noise** RF/mmWave signals by closing an optoelectronic feedback loop around a **high-quality-factor optical energy-storage element** — a long low-loss optical fibre or a high-Q **whispering-gallery-mode (WGM)** microresonator. Invented by Yao & Maleki (1994). The high optical Q yields exceptional spectral purity.
