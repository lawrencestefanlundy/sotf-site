## Physics / mechanism

IEC 61215 is the type-approval qualification standard for terrestrial photovoltaic modules: a defined sequence of accelerated stress tests applied to finished modules to establish that a design does not fail by known mechanisms within a bounded exposure. The supplied sources do not specify the test sequence, stress levels, durations or pass criteria, so no parameter values are asserted here.

The physical link between such measurements and IEC 61215 is indirect. PLQY under sustained illumination is a proxy for non-radiative recombination and for light-soaking-induced degradation in the absorber layer; TRPL lifetimes track carrier trapping. These are early screens for whether an absorber or a stabilisation additive is worth carrying forward into encapsulated modules that would then face a qualification sequence. A film that loses PLQY under 1 Sun in hours is unlikely to survive module-level stress testing, but the converse does not hold: film stability says nothing about encapsulation, interconnect, or mechanical failure modes.
